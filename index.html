My Projects 👨‍💻

Sales Performance & Business Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to provide a complete overview of business performance. It covers Sales, Customers, Items, and Salesmen, with dynamic comparisons across YTD performance, previous periods, months, weeks, and year-over-year trends — enabling managers to quickly monitor performance, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions.

Fleet Performance & Maintenance dashboard

A comprehensive Power BI solution for monitoring vehicle performance, maintenance, spare parts, and fuel consumption. It tracks each vehicle’s repair history, service visits, spare parts usage, and maintenance trends, with historical comparisons across previous visits. The dashboard also analyzes monthly and annual fuel consumption and revenue, helping identify high fuel-consuming vehicles and monitor fuel-related costs. In addition, it tracks ownership validity and expiration dates, while providing a proactive upcoming maintenance schedule to identify vehicles due for inspection in the following month.

Personal Financial Tracking

A Power BI dashboard designed to provide a clear overview of personal spending and financial activity. It analyzes spending patterns, expense categories, and the highest areas of expenditure, while tracking transactions against available assets in different currencies. Interactive currency filters allow users to quickly view and analyze transactions by USD, EGP, or other currencies, making it easier to monitor spending and financial balances.
